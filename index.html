🔪 SURGICAL MANUAL & SEED GENERATOR

WELCOME TO POST-TRAINING. Our patient — a compact 11.8M-parameter Small Language Model (Xenova/albert-base-v2) — has suffered catastrophic synaptic entanglement. Four cortical sentence probes are firing at once, and 16 vocabulary tokens are floating loose in the cranium.

HOW TO OPERATE:

  • Clamp 4 Tokens & Amputate: Select 4 related tokens on the 4×4 grid and hit 🩸 AMPUTATE (or press Enter).
  • Declassify the Hidden Probes: Sentence probes and token probabilities stay encrypted while you solve. Every time you amputate a category, its cortical banner rips open to reveal the exact [MASK] sentence probe, softmax probabilities (%), and raw logits (z).
  • One Solution, Planted Decoys: Every board is checked against the model's own logits so exactly one 4×4 split exists. Red-herring tokens score well on a second probe too — the model is genuinely torn about them, and so will you be.
  • Entanglement Tiers I–IV: Each lobe is ranked by how cleanly the model separates it from the rest of the board. I is a clean cut; IV is the most entangled lobe on the table. Sever IV first for bragging rights and bonus XP.
  • 💉 Biopsy (3 per case): Clamp a token and hit BIOPSY (B) to read its activation signature — four bars showing how hard each hidden probe α β γ δ fires for that token. Tokens with matching signatures share a lobe. Each biopsy costs XP.
  • Embedding Distance Difficulty (Δ): Select EASY (wide cosine embedding distance between sentences & words), MEDIUM, or HARD (tight cosine distance where both sentence probes and board words sit close together in embedding space).
  • 📅 Daily Case: One shared board per day, the same for every surgeon on Earth. Monday is a clean cut; the weekend is a bloodbath.
